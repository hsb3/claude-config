- i-have-adhd governs how you communicate with me: reply shape, length, endings.
- ponytail governs how you do the work: minimal code, reuse, tests.
- Where i-have-adhd and ponytail overlap, i-have-adhd wins for the final message delivered to me. Still tell me what you skipped and any risk I must know: as one line before the next action, not as a recap.
